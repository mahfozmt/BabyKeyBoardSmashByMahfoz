## 3. Audio & Sound Effects

### Procedural Sound Effects (SFX)
- **Files**: `Assets/Sounds/Sfx/*.wav` (`pop.wav`, `boing.wav`, `chime.wav`, `bell.wav`, `spring.wav`, `drum.wav`, `xylo_*.wav`)
- **License**: [Creative Commons Zero (CC0) / Public Domain](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- **Description**: Procedurally synthesized 44.1kHz 16-bit PCM waveforms (xylophone octaves, frequency modulated bubble pops, cartoon boings, and resonant bells).

### Spoken Bengali Voice Clips
- **Files**: `Assets/Sounds/Voice/*.mp3` (`num_0.mp3`–`num_9.mp3`, `let_a.mp3`–`let_z.mp3`, `cheer_*.mp3`)
- **License**: Public Domain / Educational Fair Use
- **Description**: High-clarity Bengali speech clips pronouncing numerals, alphabet characters, and encouraging toddler cheers.
